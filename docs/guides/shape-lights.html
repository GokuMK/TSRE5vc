Shape lights

Lamps on S shapes: point and spot lights in the .sd, lighting the scene as glTF lights do.

Overview

A shape light is a point or spot light that an MSTS shape (.s) carries in its .sd file: a street lamp’s light, a platform canopy’s lamps, a lantern. TSRE draws it as it draws a glTF model’s lights (KHR_lights_punctual): it lights the ground, walls, trains and the mist around it, moves with the part of the shape it hangs on, and adapts to the daylight as lamps do.

The example is TSRE’s own (section 7).

1. Where it shows

Renderer What you see
QRhi with local lights on (the default) The light lights the scene: pools of light on the ground, lit walls, light in the mist. The lamp itself shows as the shape paints it (a full-bright or night texture on its lens).
QRhi with local lights off, and the OpenGL renderer No light in the scene; a plain disc in the light’s colour marks the lamp, as signal lamps and train lamps there.

By day lamps are dimmed to a few per cent with time of day on (eyes and cameras adapt to the daylight); with time of day off they shine at full strength, so they can be placed and checked in the editor’s daylight.

2. The .sd block

One ESD_TSRELight block a light, inside the .sd’s shape ( … ) block, beside its other ESD_ entries:

text
SIMISA@@@@@@@@@@JINX0t1t______

shape ( lamppost.s
    ESD_Detail_Level ( 0 )
    ESD_Alternative_Texture ( 0 )
    ESD_TSRELight (
        Name ( "LAMP" )
        Type ( Spot )
        ShapeHierarchy ( "MAIN" )
        Position ( 1.25 5.84 0 )
        Rotation ( -90 0 0 )
        Color3F ( 1 0.82 0.6 )
        Intensity ( 60 )
        Range ( 30 )
        Cone ( 35 60 )
    )
)
Value Meaning When left out
Type Point (light all round) or Spot (a cone). Point
Intensity Its strength in candela, as glTF’s. Required: a block without it is left out.
Color3F Its colour as three numbers from 0 to 1, red, green, blue, in linear light: glTF’s color. White
ColorRGBA Or its colour as eight hex digits RRGGBBAA, as colour pickers give it; the alpha scales the intensity.
Range Metres: the light ends there. Where it fades out by its strength.
Cone A spot’s cone: the inner and outer angles from its axis, in degrees (full light inside the inner, none outside the outer). Up to 90. 0 45
ShapeHierarchy The matrix it hangs on, by name; it moves with it (an animated part’s light moves too). The shape’s root.
Position Its place in metres, in that matrix’s space. 0 0 0
Rotation Its turn in degrees about x, then y, then z, as a display’s square. No turn.
QDirection Or its turn as a quaternion x y z w, as a W file’s object or a glTF node has it.
Radius The lamp’s size in metres: it softens surfaces very close to the lamp, and is the disc’s radius. 0.05
Name Its name, shown in the Shape Viewer. None

Give one of Color3F and ColorRGBA, and one of Rotation and QDirection; with both, the later in the block is used, with a message.

3. Placing and aiming

  • Find the matrix names in the Shape Viewer’s Hierarchy window. The shape’s lights are listed there under the matrices they hang on (lights on the root at the bottom), with their type and strength.
  • A light shines along -z of its matrix, turned by Rotation or QDirection, as a glTF light along its node’s -Z. Straight down is Rotation ( -90 0 0 ); level, along +z, is Rotation ( 0 180 0 ).
  • A point light shines all round; its turn only aims its disc.
  • From a glTF model: a light node’s translation is the Position, its rotation the QDirection as it is, its light’s color, intensity and range the same values, and its spot angles (radians) in degrees. Such a light lights the scene exactly as the glTF light does.

4. Strength and reach

Lamps are rated in candela here, as in glTF. The example’s street lamp shines 60 cd down from a 6 m pole. Blender’s glTF exporter writes a point or spot light of P watts as P x 683 / (4 pi), about 54 cd a watt: a 5 W lamp in Blender is 272 cd.

A light reaches as far as its strength carries it, up to TSRE’s default reach; Range can only shorten that. For a lamp that should light farther (a floodlit yard), set the object’s light reach in the route editor’s properties of the placed object.

5. Messages

TSRE tells the shape’s author once a shape, in the messages window:

  • a block left out: no Intensity above 0, a Type other than Point or Spot;
  • both of a pair given (the later used), a ColorRGBA that is not eight hex digits (white used), a Cone out of range (the nearest used);
  • a ShapeHierarchy the shape has not (the light hangs on its root).

6. Open Rails

Open Rails skips ESD_TSRELight blocks without a message: the shape works there unchanged, without the lights. Saving a material in TSRE’s Shape Viewer keeps them.

8. Known limits

  • No shadows from lamps (as glTF lights in TSRE).
  • Lights are always on: switching by the time of day, flashing, or by a car’s ENG light conditions (for headlights) come later.
  • No directional lights (the sun lights the scene).
