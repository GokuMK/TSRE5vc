Shape emitters

Smoke and steam from S shapes: chimneys, stacks and vents in the .sd.

Overview

A shape emitter gives out smoke or steam from an MSTS shape (.s), defined in its .sd file: a house’s chimney, a factory’s stack, a steam vent. TSRE draws it with the same smoke and steam as its trains' exhausts: puffs that leave the nozzle, rise while warm, drift and spread with the wind, and thin out; steam lingers on a cold damp morning and goes quickly on a dry afternoon. Every placed object smokes on its own, so ten houses of one shape never smoke alike.

The examples are TSRE’s own (section 8).

1. Where it shows

Where What you see
The route editor’s 3D view, the simulation (QRhi, Exhaust Normal or High in the graphics settings) The smoke and steam, the simulation running or not.
The standalone Shape Viewer (QRhi) The shown shape’s emitters at their own flow (no Variation), and an engine’s or a consist’s exhaust at fixed test values: a preview without the simulation.
The OpenGL renderer, the editors' small shape views None.

On a route, the placed objects with a shape smoke: static objects, gantries, collide objects, pickups, signals, speed posts, level crossings and hazards. Track sections, dynamic track, forests, transfers and car spawners do not, nor do trains (section 9).

2. The .sd block

One ESD_TSREEmitter block an emitter, inside the .sd’s shape ( … ) block, beside its other ESD_ entries:

text
ESD_TSREEmitter (
    Name ( "POT1" )
    Position ( 4.08 7.8 -0.02 )
    Diameter ( 0.22 )
    Kind ( Exhaust )
    Flow ( 0.04 )
    ExitSpeed ( 1.5 )
    Temperature ( 120 )
    Vapour ( 0.2 )
    Soot ( 0.4 )
    Variation ( 0.3 )
)
Value Meaning When left out
Kind Its defaults for the values below (section 4): Exhaust, Stack, SafetyValves, Heating, Steam. Steam
Flow The gas it gives out, m³/s; 0 is off. The kind’s
ExitSpeed Its speed at the nozzle, m/s. The kind’s
Temperature Its temperature, °C: how fast it rises, how long steam shows. The kind’s
Vapour The share of it that is water vapour, 0 to 1; the rest is smoke. The kind’s
Soot How dark the smoke is, 0 to 1. The kind’s
Beats Puffs a second, a steam stack’s beat; 0 for an even flow. The kind’s
ColorRGBA The smoke’s colour as hex RRGGBBAA. TSRE’s own
Diameter The nozzle, m. 0.2
Variation Each placed object’s flow within plus or minus this share, the same every time for one object. 0
ShapeHierarchy The matrix it hangs on, by name; it moves with it. The shape’s root
Position Its place in metres, in that matrix’s space. 0 0 0
Rotation Its turn in degrees about x, then y, then z: the gas leaves upwards (+y) before it. Upwards
QDirection Or its turn as a quaternion x y z w, as a W file’s object has it.
On 0: off until something switches it on. 1
Name Its name, shown in the Shape Viewer. None

Give one of Rotation and QDirection; with both, the later in the block is used, with a message.

3. Placing and aiming

  • Give the Position in the shape’s own coordinates, as its vertices: the top of the chimney, a little above its rim. An emitter without a ShapeHierarchy hangs on the shape’s root, its first matrix.
  • The Shape Viewer’s Hierarchy window lists the shape’s emitters under the matrices they hang on, with their kind and flow; the standalone Shape Viewer shows their smoke.
  • The gas leaves upwards unless turned: a vent in a wall pointing along +z is Rotation ( 90 0 0 ).

4. Values, kinds and size

The kinds' defaults (TSRE’s test values for train exhausts):

Kind Flow ExitSpeed Temperature Vapour Soot Beats
Exhaust (diesel, smoke) 0.5 10 300 0.1 0.4 0
Stack (steam engine) 2 30 150 0.8 0.3 4
SafetyValves 1 100 200 1 0 0
Heating (hoses) 0.02 5 110 1 0 0
Steam 0.3 40 120 1 0 0

A house’s chimney gives far less than a diesel: Kind ( Exhaust ) with a Flow of a few hundredths and a slow ExitSpeed, as above.

A puff’s size is no value of its own: it comes from the gas. A puff is at least as wide as the nozzle and carries the flow of a thirtieth of a second (a stack’s: of its beat); it grows as it takes in the air around it, faster from a fast nozzle and in wind. A large stack is a wide Diameter with a large Flow.

Variation keeps a row of houses from smoking evenly: each placed object takes its own share of the flow, fixed by its UiD.

5. Limits

Scenery’s smoke and steam are kept apart from the trains': 1,000 puffs at once at Normal, 3,000 at High (the trains have 3,000 and 10,000 of their own). Objects within 2,000 m of the camera smoke; when the scenery’s puffs are full, the nearest emitters keep theirs. Objects smoke whether they are in view or not, so a plume is there when the camera turns. An object whose shape draws nothing past its last level (ESD_TSREDistanceLevelExtension ( None ), Shape lights, section 4) gives out none there.

6. Messages

TSRE tells the shape’s author once a shape, in the messages window, and again after the shape is reloaded: a Kind it does not know (Steam used), both Rotation and QDirection, a ColorRGBA that is not eight hex digits, a ShapeHierarchy the shape has not (the emitter hangs on its root).

7. Open Rails

Open Rails skips ESD_TSREEmitter blocks without a message: the shape works there unchanged, without the smoke.

8. Example

Shape emitter example: chimney pots on a stock cottage, and a test shape smoking in a row on a route.

9. Known limits

  • An emitter on a locomotive’s or wagon’s shape gives out nothing yet: a car smokes from its ENG’s emitters only. Binding the shape’s emitters to the ENG’s exhaust comes later.
  • No emitters in glTF models yet.
  • Emitters are on or off by On only: switching by the time of day or by events comes later.
  • No size value; no smoke on the OpenGL renderer.
